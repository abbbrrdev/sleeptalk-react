## Описание (Description) · 2160/4000
```
What do you say when you're asleep? Tonight you find out. 
Put your phone by the bed, tap Start night and sleep. In the morning SleepTalk hands you your night as a tiny zine: the best quote, the snoring, the laughs.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap Start night and leave the phone on the nightstand (charger in)
• SleepTalk records all night but keeps only the moments with sound
• Silence is thrown away: you get clips, not 8 hours of tape
• Apple's on-device sound classifier sorts every clip into talking, snoring, laughing and coughing

YOUR MORNING REPORT
• The best quote of the night, front-page style
• Things you said, your snore score and every laugh
• A timeline of the whole night
• Clips you can play right there

WHAT DID I SAY? (PRO)
Turn sleep talk into text and finally read your mumbling back.

SHARE THE GOOD STUFF
Make a quote card with the audio and send it to friends, the group chat or your stories. 

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The full night recording is deleted after processing
• Clips stay only on your iPhone
• No account, no cloud, no sign-up

FREE
• Record every night
• Morning report and timeline
• The 3 loudest clips each night
• Sharing
• Your last 3 reports

SLEEPTALK PRO
• All clips, every night
• "What did I say?" transcription
• Snore score trends, week by week
• The full archive of reports
• Clips kept forever

GOOD TO KNOW
• SleepTalk is for fun and curiosity. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. If you snore loudly with pauses in breathing, talk to a doctor.
• Sharing a room? Let the others know before you start recording.
```
