Questions

Before lights out.

Will it drain my battery?

Recording all night uses about as much as playing music quietly. Plug in the charger — SleepTalk reminds you.

Does it record my partner?

It hears everything in the room, so yes, their sounds can end up in your clips. Tell them before you start, and only share clips with their OK.

How much storage does it use?

Only the clips are kept — usually 1–5 MB a night. The full recording is deleted after the night is processed.

Can it tell me if I have sleep apnea?

No. SleepTalk is for curiosity, not diagnosis. Loud snoring with pauses or gasping can be a sign of sleep apnea — that's a question for a doctor.

Android?

Not yet. iPhone first.

How do I cancel?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SleepTalk. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal and you won't be charged. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.
