Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026 · Applies to the SleepTalk iOS app and this website
The short version
  • SleepTalk has no accounts and no servers. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • While a night is running, the app records audio from the microphone, including in the background. The full recording is stored only on your iPhone and is deleted as soon as the night is processed.
  • Only short clips of detected sounds are kept, on your iPhone. They are sorted by Apple's sound classifier on the device.
  • If you tap “What did I say?”, the clip is transcribed with Apple's speech recognition — on device when available, otherwise by Apple.
  • No audio, clips or transcripts are ever uploaded to us. Audio leaves your phone only if you share a clip.
  • The App sends anonymous usage stats (screens and taps — never audio, clips or transcripts) to PostHog so we can see where people get stuck. You can turn this off in Settings.

1. Who we are

SleepTalk (“SleepTalk”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS app that records sounds you make during sleep. It is developed by an independent developer based in Astana, Kazakhstan, who is the controller of any personal data described here.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier (IDFA) or browsing history. The App contains no ads and no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs; the only analytics is the anonymous usage statistics described below, which you can turn off. We do not sell or share personal data and do not track you across apps or websites.

3. Microphone and overnight recording

  • The microphone is used only after you tap “Start night” and stops when you end the night. While a night runs, recording continues if the screen locks or the App is in the background (iOS shows the recording indicator).
  • The overnight recording is saved temporarily in the App's private storage. After you end the night, the App cuts short clips around detected sounds and then deletes the full recording.
  • Clips are sorted into talking, snoring, laughing and coughing by Apple's SoundAnalysis framework, which runs entirely on your device.
  • Recordings may capture other people in the room (for example a partner). Please let them know before you start a night.

4. Transcription (“What did I say?”)

When you tap “What did I say?”, the selected clip is processed by Apple's Speech framework. If your iPhone has the on-device model for the chosen language, transcription happens on the device. Otherwise iOS may send the clip to Apple for transcription, under the Apple Privacy Policy. The resulting text is stored only in the App.

6. Anonymous usage analytics

To see where people get stuck — for example, during onboarding — and to improve the App, the App sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (PostHog Inc.). Our PostHog project is hosted on PostHog's EU cloud, so this data is processed in the EU.

  • What is sent: a random install ID created by the App (no account, no advertising identifier, no name or email), the screens you view and actions such as “onboarding step”, “paywall shown” or “trial started” — with numbers or preset values only — plus app version, iOS version, device model and app language.
  • What is never sent: audio, clips, transcripts or quotes — nothing you say in your sleep. Location lookup by IP address is turned off (no GeoIP).
  • What it's not used for: advertising, tracking you across other apps or websites, or selling data.
  • Retention: events are kept for PostHog's default retention period and then deleted.
  • Turn it off: in the App, go to Settings → “Share anonymous usage stats”. Once it's off, no more events are sent.

PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy.

7. Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple; we never see card details. RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) verifies subscriptions and receives a random anonymous app user ID (the same random install ID used for usage analytics, so subscription events can be matched to it), your App Store purchase history and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model, App Store country, IP address) — never audio, clips or transcripts. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

8. This website

No cookies, no analytics. Your language choice is kept in local storage. Hosting (Vercel) may log IP addresses; fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

9. Retention and deletion

Everything the App stores is on your device and is deleted when you delete a night, reset the App or delete the App. Anonymous usage events are kept by PostHog for its default retention period. You can revoke microphone and speech recognition access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Do not use it to record children without the consent of a parent or guardian.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “On Personal Data and Its Protection”), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Since your data stays on your device, you can exercise most of them in the App. You can stop usage analytics at any time in Settings. For RevenueCat or PostHog data, contact us.

12. Security and transfers

On-device data is protected by iOS sandboxing and device encryption. Connections to RevenueCat and PostHog use HTTPS. RevenueCat processes data in the USA with appropriate safeguards (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses); PostHog usage analytics are processed in the EU.

13. Changes and contact

We'll update the date above if this policy changes and announce significant changes in the App. Questions? Use the support link on SleepTalk's App Store page, or email .

3. Микрофон и запись ночью

  • Микрофон используется только после нажатия «Начать ночь» и выключается, когда ты завершаешь ночь. Пока ночь идёт, запись продолжается при заблокированном экране и в фоне (iOS показывает индикатор записи).

  • В запись могут попасть другие люди в комнате (например, партнёр). Пожалуйста, предупреди их перед стартом ночи.

Когда ты нажимаешь «Что я говорю?», выбранный клип обрабатывает фреймворк Speech от Apple. Если на iPhone есть модель выбранного языка, расшифровка идёт на устройстве. Иначе iOS может отправить клип в Apple для расшифровки согласно Политике конфиденциальности Apple. Получившийся текст хранится только в Приложении.

6. Анонимная аналитика использования

Чтобы понимать, где люди застревают (например, во время онбординга), и делать Приложение лучше, оно отправляет анонимные события использования в PostHog (PostHog Inc.). Наш проект PostHog размещён в европейском облаке PostHog, поэтому эти данные обрабатываются в ЕС.

  • Что отправляется: случайный идентификатор установки, который создаёт само Приложение (без аккаунта, рекламного идентификатора, имени и email), какие экраны ты открываешь и действия вроде «шаг онбординга», «показан экран подписки» или «начат пробный период» — только числа или значения из заданного списка, — а также версия приложения и iOS, модель устройства и язык приложения.
  • Что никогда не отправляется: аудио, клипы, расшифровки и цитаты — ничего из того, что ты говоришь во сне. Определение местоположения по IP-адресу выключено (без GeoIP).
  • Для чего не используется: для рекламы, отслеживания тебя в других приложениях и на сайтах и продажи данных.
  • Срок хранения: события хранятся в течение стандартного срока хранения PostHog, затем удаляются.
  • Как выключить: в Приложении: Настройки → «Делиться анонимной статистикой». После этого события больше не отправляются.

Политика конфиденциальности PostHog: posthog.com/privacy.
